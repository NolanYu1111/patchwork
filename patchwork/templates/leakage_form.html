What it checks

Label leakage. Each column's out-of-fold AUROC on its own. A field that predicts the diagnosis almost perfectly, or is written by the person who made the diagnosis, is flagged.

Patient overlap. The same person in training and test inflates every score.

Association flips. Whether each category leans the same way in both cohorts. In our study head-and-neck leaned eczema at one site and psoriasis at the other.
