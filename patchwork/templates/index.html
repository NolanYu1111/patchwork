Why this exists

Dermatology AI is often trained on lighter skin and tested on data from the same hospital. Those tests can look excellent and still hide failures. In our study, published at the IEEE BigData 2026 High School Symposium, two conclusions flipped when we tested on outside data:

  • 0.711 vs 0.503AUROC on Fitzpatrick I–IV vs V–VI on the external cohort. The darker-skin result was no better than a coin flip, and our internal tests had shown no gap at all.
  • 0.699 → 0.451Body-site metadata looked useful internally and fell to chance externally, because site patterns reflect who visits a clinic, not the disease.
  • 0.795 → 0.894Clinician-recorded descriptors inflated accuracy by leaking the diagnosis they were written alongside.

Patchwork packages that evaluation protocol so any team can run it, whatever the condition and whatever the model.
